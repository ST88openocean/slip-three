WHY I AM ASKING YOU TO DOWNLOAD THIS

This site sits on a free host under a name that isn't mine. That is fine until it isn't. One complaint, one dead account, and everything above is gone in an afternoon, and I have no way to put it back.

So take a copy. Not the bit you found interesting — all of it, including the capture, which is the only part that proves anything. Put it on a disk that is not in the same building as you, and if somebody asks for it, give it to them.

I am not asking anyone to trust me. That is what the fingerprints are for. Check yours against the list:

shasum -a 256 slip-three-01.mp3
certutil -hashfile slip-three-01.mp3 SHA256

First line is macOS or Linux. Second is Windows. If the number that comes back matches the one above, nobody has been at it in between.
