# Slip Three

A serialised audio drama and alternate reality game.

Ten transmissions. One voice, broadcasting from open water on a six-second
satellite window, counting down to something.

- **Listen / subscribe:** https://ST88openocean.github.io/slip-three
- **Feed:** https://ST88openocean.github.io/slip-three/podcast.xml
- **Schedule:** Tuesdays, 03:00 UTC

Paste the feed URL into any podcast app.

## This is fiction

SLIP THREE is a work of fiction and an alternate reality game.

Every character, vessel, organisation, agency, callsign, coordinate, package
identifier and date in it is invented — including the Meridian Coastal
Authority, Corran Quay, Kestrel Bank and the *Ostrava Point*. It is not a real
disclosure, not a real distress broadcast, and no real person, company or
public body is depicted, accused, or referred to.

Nothing in the story asks you to visit any location, contact any person, or
identify anybody in the real world. Everything needed to follow it is published
here.
